Hub Grid Chain

Play 1v1

Take turns extending one shared chain — whoever runs out of clock, or can't (or won't) continue it, loses the round. The room host sets sudden death for the round below before creating it; it applies to both players. Every 1v1 round is timed, with the per-turn clock shrinking as the chain grows.
